## Research

**Do Hourly Carbon Signals Overstate Scope 2 Reductions? Average vs. Marginal Emission Factors in Carbon-Aware Production Scheduling with Taiwan Grid Data**
First author, with Nguyen Ha Phuong Du and Dam My Tran. Under review, 2026 International Conference on New Management Challenges in the ESG Era.

- Estimated hourly average and marginal emission factors for Taiwan's grid from public data.
- Scheduled 13 flow shops with constraint programming (OR-Tools CP-SAT) against a time-of-use cost baseline.
- In the coal-heavy grid, average factors reported 1.8–2.8 times the marginal CO₂ cut; most real savings came from removing idle machine energy.

### Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Detection
Team project, extended individually. Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow/Keras, YOLOv8.

- YOLOv8 + Haar + CNN pipeline with PERCLOS-based alerts.
- Subject-level split: 95.8% closed-eye recall on people the model has never seen.
- Found the Haar mouth ratio is constant by design; temporal smoothing made live yawn detection work.
