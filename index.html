Research / Operations & ESG

Do Hourly Carbon Signals Overstate Scope 2 Reductions?

First-author paper (with Nguyen Ha Phuong Du and Dam My Tran), under review at the 2026 International Conference on New Management Challenges in the ESG Era. I estimated hourly average and marginal emission factors for Taiwan's grid from public data and scheduled 13 flow shops with constraint programming against a time-of-use cost baseline. In the coal-heavy grid, average factors reported 1.8–2.8 times the marginal CO₂ cut; most real savings came from removing idle machine energy.

Business Analysis / EdTech

TrueCareer — Career Guidance Platform

Individual project: a platform that helps Vietnamese high school students choose a major and talk to career experts through 1-on-1 video calls. I mapped the customer journey, built a high-fidelity Axure prototype and wrote user stories with business rules, order states, permissions and Given/When/Then acceptance criteria. A K-Means prototype on 800 synthetic profiles tested the recommendation logic; I showed its high label agreement (ARI 0.85) only reflects how the synthetic data was made.

Computer Vision / AI

Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Detection

Team project, extended individually. A YOLOv8 + Haar + CNN pipeline detects closed eyes and yawning from a webcam and raises PERCLOS-based alerts. I fixed the evaluation with a subject-level split (95.8% closed-eye recall on unseen people), found the Haar mouth ratio is constant by design, added temporal smoothing that made live yawn detection work, and cut the eye-closure alert from about 3 s to 1.5 s. Runs at 20 FPS on a laptop CPU (26 with GPU).
