About Me

Hi, I'm Bao Tran. I like the part of a software project where a messy real-world problem becomes something a team can actually build. That means talking to the people who know the business, mapping how users move through the product, and writing it all down clearly enough that nothing important is left to guesswork.

In my TrueCareer project, I analyzed the problem, mapped the customer journey, designed the prototype and wrote user stories with business rules, states, permissions and acceptance criteria. As Business Analyst, I mapped the customer journey, designed a 19-screen Axure prototype and wrote user stories with business rules, order states, permissions and Given/When/Then acceptance criteria. Computer Vision / AI

Real-Time Driver Drowsiness Detection

A real-time system that uses YOLOv8 face detection, Haar Cascades and two lightweight CNNs to detect closed eyes and yawning from webcam video, then triggers a visual/audio alert. Trained on ~80k eye and ~5k mouth images, it reached 86% eye-state and 95% yawn validation accuracy at 15.7 FPS on a CPU-only laptop.
